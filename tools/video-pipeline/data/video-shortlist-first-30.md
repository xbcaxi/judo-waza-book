## F. Sources found for validating the edge list itself

- judoinfo.com/combine/ : a curated list of renraku and kaeshi pairs plus groundwork combinations. Use to confirm or drop edges in judo-sequences.md.
- British Judo copy of the IJF/Kodokan Classification of Judo (PDF): canonical names, kanji, and three-letter codes for every technique including the gaeshi counters. Good for the `kanji` table and the alias list.
- Kodokan Definitions of Judo Techniques (2022 PDF, via kdkjudo.org): one-line official definitions.
- Nicola Fairbrother, Learn 20 Combinations: Advanced Judo (Koka Kids): aimed at junior judoka, which matches the Waza Book audience; her substack lists the combinations covered.
