# Photography shot list: the own-image programme

Generated 2026-08-31 from the technique data, for the image replacement
programme: every third-party and unknown image replaced by our own
photography, which NOTICE.md and CONTRIBUTING.md have always asked for. Two lists below: the 67 replacements first, then the
43 techniques that have never had an image, which are worth shooting in the
same sessions since the pair is already on the mat.

## House style, fixed before the first frame

- One frame set per technique: throws get three (kuzushi, the turn-in, the
  throw mid-flight); hold-downs two (the entry, the hold secured); strangles
  and joint locks two (the entry grip, the applied position with uke tapping).
  Replacing a three-panel illustration means three photographs, laid out by
  the site, not a collage baked into one file.
- Same mat, same backdrop, same pair throughout a family of techniques. Tori
  in blue, uke in white, every session, so a reader always knows who is who.
- Shoot landscape, room to crop; the delivery format and sizes are the
  website repo's docs/images.md. Keep the RAW/original files outside the
  repository; commit the derived WebP only.
- Faces are fine (all demonstrators are adults) and head position is part of
  several techniques. Every demonstrator signs the model release once:
  use in the Waza Book reference in all its editions, app editions included,
  free or paid, credited or not at our discretion.
- The "What the frames must show" column is the technique's own published
  key points: if the photo does not show that, it is not the right frame.
  The full step-by-step is on each technique page and is the shot brief.
- As each image lands: `provenance: "own"`, `credit: "Waza Book"`, alt text
  written fresh for the photograph (it will no longer be an "illustrated
  sequence"). The counts on /rights/ and in NOTICE.md follow on their own.
- Ten techniques below are marked banned in shiai. Photographing them is
  fine and their pages exist exactly so a judoka can recognise them, but
  pose the dangerous ones rather than throwing them live: kani-basami and
  kawazu-gake set up and held, not completed, and do-jime (prohibited even
  in randori) shown as the position alone. The rest are ordinary techniques
  outside competition and can be shot normally.

## Priority: sessions cover a family at a time

"Grades" counts how many grading-scheme grades name the technique, so it is
the load-bearing order: a technique on many syllabuses is seen by more
readers. Suggested session order: ashi-waza (largest), te-waza + koshi-waza
(one session), osaekomi + shime (one mat session on the ground), kansetsu +
sacrifices last.
